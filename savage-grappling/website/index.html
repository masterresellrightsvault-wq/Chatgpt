The format

You won't lose once
and go home

Most comps give you one match if you draw badly. At Savage you're in a pool of up to five in your belt, age and weight, and you fight everyone in it. More mat time for your entry fee, and a fairer way to find the best in the pool.

  • ScoringTakedown 2, sweep 2, knee on belly 2, guard pass 3, mount 4, back 4. No advantages.
  • DivisionsKids, teens and adults. Gi and No-Gi, white to black belt, plus absolutes.
  • Podium1st wins the "I proved it on the mats" tee. 2nd and 3rd get medals.
  • TeamsBest Overall Academy trophies for Adults and for Kids & Teens, with live standings on Smoothcomp.
  • SafetyTrained medics on site and experienced referees on every mat.

FAQ

Questions

Is this a good first competition?

Yes. Because it's a round robin you get 3–4 matches even if you lose your first, and divisions are split by belt, age and weight. Most first-timers leave having learned more in one day than in a month of training.

Can I register on the day?

No. You must pre-register on Smoothcomp. Entries and division changes close on Friday 2 October at 10am.

When and where do I weigh in?

Weigh-ins are on the day at the venue. Weigh in at least 60 minutes before your first match.

Can I enter both Gi and No-Gi?

Yes. Your first division is the normal price and the second is $25, so $100 for both as an adult.

What if I need to withdraw?

The full-refund window for 4 October closed on 25 September. You can still cancel or edit your entry until 2 October at 10am. Check Smoothcomp for the exact policy for each event.

Can my family watch?

Yes. Spectators are $10 at the door. Kids and teens compete throughout the day.

What should I bring?

Your gi and/or rash guard and shorts, a mouthguard, water and real food for a long day, and thongs or slides for off the mat.
